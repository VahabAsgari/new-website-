How we work

Every strategy at Joveyra exists twice: on paper, in the hand of the person who derived it, and in the model that trades it.

#1 Research

Observed before it is modelled

We start from data, the way Galileo started from four points of light beside Jupiter, recorded night after night.

#2 Models

Derived by hand

Each pricing model begins as mathematics on paper: derived, argued over and proven before it becomes code.

#3 Execution

Traded by the model

Our pricing models place every trade, on time and without discretion, impulse or fatigue.

#4 Risk

Measured before return

Positions are sized by what they can lose. Risk is measured continuously, every moment a market is open.

#5 Infrastructure

Built, not bought

Data pipelines, research tools and execution systems are made in-house. We do not run what we cannot explain.

#6 Oversight

Answerable to people

Nothing runs unobserved. Every model has an author, and every author answers for what the model does.

People

Most of our researchers were first noticed at international olympiads in mathematics and physics. We hire for how people think, and we judge that by how they solve problems.

Questions

Does Joveyra manage outside capital?
Joveyra manages capital for a small number of qualified investors. We do not solicit; new relationships begin with an introduction.
Do you publish performance?
No. Performance is shared in full with investors and, under confidentiality, with qualified prospective investors. More on the firm.
Who decides what to trade?
Pricing models place every trade. Researchers design, test and supervise the models; no trade is placed at someone's discretion.
Who works at Joveyra?
Mostly physicists and mathematicians, many of them olympiad medalists, with engineers who build the systems the models run on.
What is the Joveyra Platform?
A trading platform we are building for institutions and professional traders in the Middle East, available in English and Persian. See the platform.
How do I apply?
Solve the problem above and send your solution to careers@joveyra.com. There is no form and no deadline.
