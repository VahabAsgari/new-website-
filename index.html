Joveyra

Joveyra is a quantitative investment firm. Our researchers, most of them physicists and mathematicians, build the pricing models that trade our capital.

The models place every trade; the people who wrote them answer for each one.

I

Research

Every model begins as a question about how a price is formed. Researchers work it out on paper, test it against decades of data, and discard most of what they try.

Pl. II · Epikouros · Roman copy of a Greek original, 2nd c. CE · The Met

II

Execution

The models place every trade, in every market we hold, at any hour. No position is opened or closed at someone's discretion.

Pl. III · Head from a herm · Roman, 1st–2nd c. CE · The Met

III

Supervision

Each model has an author, and the author answers for what it does. Risk is measured continuously, and positions are sized by what they can lose.

Pl. IV · Head of a Greek general · Roman, 1st–2nd c. CE · The Met

IV

People

Most of our researchers were first noticed at the international olympiads in mathematics and physics. We hire for how people think, and we judge that by how they solve problems.

A problem

Io, Europa and Ganymede orbit Jupiter in 1.769, 3.551 and 7.155 days. Writing λ for each moon's mean longitude, show that λIo − 3λEuropa + 2λGanymede stays constant, and find its value.

Send your solution to careers@joveyra.com. There is no form and no deadline.

V

Joveyra Platform

We are building a trading platform for institutions and professional traders in the Middle East, in English and Persian, on the infrastructure that runs our own capital.

Questions

Does Joveyra manage outside capital?
Joveyra manages capital for a small number of qualified investors. We do not solicit; new relationships begin with an introduction.
Do you publish performance?
No. Performance is shared in full with investors and, under confidentiality, with qualified prospective investors.
Who decides what to trade?
Pricing models place every trade. Researchers design, test and supervise the models; no trade is placed at someone's discretion.
Who works here?
Mostly physicists and mathematicians, many of them olympiad medalists, with engineers who build the systems the models run on.
What is Joveyra Platform?
A trading platform we are building for institutions and professional traders in the Middle East, available in English and Persian. More on the platform.
How do I apply?
Solve the problem above and send your solution to careers@joveyra.com. There is no form and no deadline.
