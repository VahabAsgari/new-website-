IIIMethod

The hand and the model.

Every strategy at Joveyra lives twice: once on paper, in the hand of the person who derived it, and once in the machine that executes it. Neither is allowed to forget the other.

  • i

    Written by hand

    Each model begins as mathematics on paper — derived, argued over, proven — long before it becomes code.

  • ii

    Executed by the model

    Trades are made by our pricing models. Without impulse, without fatigue, without exception.

  • iii

    Answerable to us

    Nothing runs unobserved. Every model has an author, and every author answers for what the model does.

VPeople

Found at the edge of the solvable.

Most of the people at Joveyra were first noticed at international olympiads in mathematics and physics — at an age when the hardest problem in the room was the only one worth attempting.

We did not hire them for what they knew. We hired them for how they think: patiently, rigorously, and without needing to be told that a problem is difficult.

We do not publish their names. Their work is the signature.
