IIOrigin

A word, a book, a region.

In the ninth century, a mathematician from Khwarazm wrote the book that gave the world the word algebra. His own name, carried into Latin, became the word algorithm.

The instrument-makers of this region measured the heavens with a precision the rest of the world took centuries to equal. Every model that trades a market today descends from that lineage.

We intend to set that standard again — here, for the people of this region, on their own terms.
