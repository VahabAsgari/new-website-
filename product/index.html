Where the word began

In the ninth century, Muhammad ibn Musa al-Khwarizmi wrote the book that gave the world the word algebra. Translated into Latin, his own name became algoritmi, and then the word every trading system now runs on.

The instrument-makers of this region measured the sky with a precision the rest of the world took centuries to equal. We are building a platform that holds itself to that standard, for the people of this region, on their own terms.

What it does

One surface for the markets of the region and the world beyond them.

#1 Terminal

Every market, one surface

Prices, depth, news and analytics for regional and global markets, designed to be read at a glance.

#2 Execution

Orders that arrive in time

Direct, low-latency order routing built on the infrastructure we use for our own trading.

#3 Data

Every number traceable

Clean, complete and continuous market data, with every value traceable to its source.

#4 Models

Research-grade analytics

Pricing and risk tools drawn from the research that runs Joveyra's own capital.

#5 Sovereignty

Your data stays here

Hosted in the region and governed by the region's law. Security is designed in, not added on.

#6 Language

English and Persian

Every screen written in both languages from the start, with Persian set right to left as it should be.

Questions

The exchanges of the region first, with global equities, rates, currencies and commodities alongside them.
Which languages?
English and Persian, both written natively. More languages of the region will follow.
