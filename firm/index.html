The Firm

How Joveyra conducts itself, what it discloses, and to whom.

Investor enquiries Principles

What we disclose

We do not advertise results. We account for them, in full, to the people entitled to see them.

Performance

To investors, in full

Investors receive complete performance and risk reporting. Qualified prospective investors can review it under confidentiality.

Positions

Not in public

We do not publish holdings or strategies. They are discussed with investors, and with regulators where required.

Commentary

None

We do not comment on markets, positions or results in public, and our people do not either.

Principles

Three rules that every model and every person at Joveyra works under.

#1 Risk

Loss before gain

Every position is sized by what it can lose before what it can earn. Return is the result of that discipline, never the excuse for breaking it.

#2 Discipline

The model keeps time

Once a model is approved it trades as written. People change models through research and review, never in the moment.

#3 Discretion

One face to the world

The Moon always shows Earth the same face. What we show in public is this website; everything else belongs to our investors.

Questions

Are you accepting new investors?
Occasionally, and only by introduction. Write to office@joveyra.com with the name of the person who introduced you.
Can I see your track record?
Qualified prospective investors can review the full record under a confidentiality agreement, after an introduction.
Who decides what to trade?
Pricing models place every trade. Researchers design, test and supervise them; no trade is placed at someone's discretion.
Is the platform part of the fund?
No. The Joveyra Platform is a separate product for traders in the Middle East. It is built on the same infrastructure, but client activity on the platform is kept apart from the firm's own trading.
