The Firm

Joveyra is a quantitative investment firm. Pricing models trade its capital; the researchers who build them supervise every one.

The Firm

Joveyra is run by the people who build its models. Most of them came from physics and mathematics, and many were first noticed at the international olympiads.

Every model that trades our capital has a named author, and the author answers for what it does.

II

Principles

  1. I

    Loss before gain

    Positions are sized by what they can lose before what they might earn.

  2. II

    The model keeps time

    Models trade on schedule, without impulse or fatigue. People decide what the models are; the models decide when to trade.

  3. III

    Discretion

    We do not publish, advertise or comment. New relationships begin with an introduction.

III

What we disclose

  1. 01

    Performance

    To investors, in full. To qualified prospective investors, under confidentiality.

  2. 02

    Positions

    Not in public.

  3. 03

    Commentary

    None. We do not comment on markets.

Questions

How do I become an investor?
By introduction only. We do not solicit, and we do not accept capital through intermediaries we have not met.
Can I see the track record?
The record is available to qualified prospective investors, under confidentiality. Write to office@joveyra.com.
How does the firm relate to Joveyra Platform?
The platform is a separate business. It runs on the same infrastructure as the fund. More on the platform.
