IIConduct

Three rules we do not bend.

iRisk first

What it can lose

Every position is sized by what it can lose before what it can earn. Return is the consequence of discipline, never its excuse.

iiIndependence

Built, not bought

Our models, our infrastructure, our data pipelines — made in-house, understood end to end. We do not run what we cannot explain.

iiiSilence

Nothing in public

We do not comment on markets, positions or results in public. What we know, we keep for those we answer to.

IIISpeed

Motion, decomposed.

Pl. VIII — Animal Locomotion, plate 596. Eadweard Muybridge, 1887

In the 1870s a horse was photographed in a sequence of frames to settle a question the eye could not answer: whether, for one instant, all four hooves leave the ground.

They do. Our systems live in that instant — in the frames between, where a price is still becoming a price. We do not say how fast. We say it is fast enough to see what others infer.
